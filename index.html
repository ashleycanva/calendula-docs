Shopify theme for med spas, aesthetic clinics and skincare lines

Lumen documentation

Lumen is built for clinics that sell treatments and a skincare line from one site. This guide walks through every setting, section and block in the order you'll meet them in the theme editor.

Contact support

01

Getting started

Install the theme

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Open the Theme Store, find Lumen and select Try theme or Buy theme. Lumen is added to your theme library.
  3. Select Customize to open the theme editor. Your current live theme is not changed until you select Publish.
Before you change codeAlways duplicate the theme first (Online Store → Themes → … → Duplicate) and work on the copy. Theme updates can't be merged into files you've edited by hand.

Set up in this order

  1. Booking: in Theme settings → Booking, paste the link to your booking software. Every Book button in the theme uses it.
  2. Logo and menu: in the Header section, upload a logo and choose your main menu.
  3. Colors and type: in Theme settings → Colors and Typography.
  4. Home page: replace the placeholder images and text in each section.
  5. Products: add the product metafields described in Product content, then turn off Show demo product copy.
  6. Filters: choose which filters appear with the free Search & Discovery app (see Collections and search).

How Lumen is organized

Every page is built from sections, and many sections hold blocks. In the theme editor you can add, remove, reorder and hide sections and blocks on any page. App blocks from installed apps can be added to the product page and to the Featured product section, and a Custom Liquid section or block is available for small additions.

Open the theme editor and select the gear icon (Theme settings). These settings apply across the whole store.

Lumen's palette is sand, indigo and copper. Copper is the accent and is used sparingly: links on hover, labels and small highlights.

04

Home page sections

All of these sections can be added to any page that supports sections. Until you add your own images, sections show neutral placeholder art.

Hero (split)

A large heading on the left and a full-height panel on the right.

  • Heading: separate lines with a vertical bar, for example Results you can see.|Care you can feel. The second line is set in copper italic.
  • Show: choose Video loop, Photograph or The plate drawing (an illustrated line drawing). Videos play muted on a loop; upload them through the video picker.
  • Fact blocks: up to four small figures under the heading, such as years in practice or treatments performed. Numbers count up when they come into view.
  • Ticker: a scrolling line of treatments. Separate items with commas and add a duration with a middle dot: Microneedling · 60 min.

Recommended image size: 1400 x 1750px or larger, portrait.

Treatment menu

A ruled list of treatments. Each Treatment block has a name, what it's for, duration, downtime, an optional starting price and an optional link to a details page.

Concerns map

Helps visitors start from their concern (acne, pigmentation, fine lines) rather than a treatment name. Each Concern block links to a treatment and a product. If you don't pick a product, the text in Product (if none selected) is shown instead.

Before / after

Comparison sliders that visitors drag to reveal the result. Each Comparison block takes a before photo, an after photo, the treatment and the protocol (for example, "3 sessions, 4 weeks apart").

Patient photosOnly use photographs you have written consent to publish, taken in the same light and framing. Use the Disclaimer field for any wording your regulator requires. Recommended size: 4:3, at least 1200 x 900px.

Practitioners

Portraits of your team. Each Practitioner block has a portrait, name, title, registration (for example a license number) and the treatments they lead. On phones the cards become a swipeable row. Portraits look best at 4:5, at least 900 x 1125px.

Routine builder

A morning and evening skincare routine with AM / PM tabs. Each Step block sets the time of day, the step name, why it matters and the product. Steps are numbered in the order they appear in the editor.

Shop the line

A grid of products from one collection. Choose the collection and how many products to show.

Image with text

A photograph or video beside a heading, text and an optional link. Turn on Image on the right to swap the sides. A video takes precedence over the image.

Testimonials

Quotes from patients. Each Quote block has the quote, the person's name and the treatment they had. On phones quotes become a swipeable row.

FAQ

Questions and answers that open and close. Add one Question block per question.

Journal

The latest entries from a blog. Choose the blog and how many entries to show.

Book

The closing call to book, on a dark background, with your booking link, phone number and opening hours. Its anchor is #book.

Featured product

One product that can be bought without leaving the page, with the same blocks as the product page (see below).

Product recommendations

Available on product pages only. Choose Complementary to show products you've paired with the Search & Discovery app, or Related for products Shopify chooses automatically. If there's nothing to recommend, the section hides itself.

Custom Liquid

Paste an app snippet or a small piece of Liquid or HTML. Code added here isn't covered by theme support (see Support policy).

Media

Lumen shows every image, video, YouTube or Vimeo link and 3D model you add to a product. The first is shown large. When a customer chooses a variant that has its own image, that image moves to the front. Turn on Show 3D models first to lead with a 3D model when a product has one. Set focal points in Shopify (on the image in Content → Files) to control how images are cropped.

Pickup availability

When local pickup is turned on for one of your locations (Settings → Shipping and delivery → Local pickup), the Buy buttons block shows whether the selected variant can be picked up, with a list of locations.

06

Collections, search and filters

Collection pages show the collection's title, description and image, a filter sidebar and the product grid. The search page uses the same filters and sorting for product results, followed by matching pages and journal entries.

Choose your filters

  1. Install the free Shopify Search & Discovery app.
  2. In the app, open Filters and add the ones you want: availability, price, product type, vendor, tags, variant options or metafields such as skin type or concern.
  3. Filters appear automatically on collection and search pages, in the order you set in the app.

Filters apply as soon as a customer changes them, without reloading the page.

Collection settingWhat it controls
EyebrowThe small label above the collection title.
Show collection descriptionThe description you write on the collection.
Show collection imageThe collection's image beside the title.
Products per pageFrom 8 to 48.

Search shows suggestions as customers type in the header. Use the arrow keys to move through suggestions and Enter to open one.

07

Cart and checkout

The cart page lists each item with its price, quantity, variant, any subscription plan and any discounts. Changing a quantity or removing an item updates the cart straight away. Order discounts, the total and tax information appear in the summary.

  • Enable order notes: lets customers leave a note, for example allergies or delivery instructions.
  • Dynamic checkout buttons such as Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay appear under the Check out button when you've turned them on in Settings → Payments.

Lumen calls the cart "Bag" in the storefront to match its tone.

08

Pages, blog and gift cards

Contact page

  1. Go to Online Store → Pages and create a page called Contact.
  2. Under Theme template, choose contact.

Messages are sent to your store's sender email. To change the address, go to Settings → Notifications.

Journal (blog)

The journal lists entries with their image, date and excerpt. Articles show the author (which you can hide), tags and comments when comments are enabled for the blog. If you moderate comments, customers are told their comment will appear once approved.

Gift cards

The gift card page shows the balance, the code with a Copy button, a QR code for in-store redemption, Add to Apple Wallet and a Print button.

Password page

While your store is password protected, visitors see your store name, your password message and a password field.

09

Accessibility and motion

  • Every control works with a keyboard and has a visible focus outline, including dropdown menus, filters and search suggestions.
  • Text colors are chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast against the default palette.
  • Buttons and links on phones are at least 44 x 44px.
  • Animations respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting, and you can turn them off for everyone in Theme settings → Motion and navigation.

Add alt text to your images in Shopify so screen readers can describe them.

10

Frequently asked questions

Can customers book appointments inside the store?

Lumen links every Book button to your existing booking software, so appointments, deposits and reminders stay where your team already manages them. Add the link in Theme settings → Booking.

Why are my Book buttons missing?

Book buttons are hidden when the booking link is empty. Add it in Theme settings → Booking → Booking link.

Why do my products show text I didn't write?

That's the demo product copy, which only appears for Lumen's six demo products. Turn off Theme settings → Layout → Show demo product copy, and add your own content with the product metafields.

How do I add filters such as skin type or concern?

Create a product metafield (for example custom.skin_type), fill it in on your products, then add it as a filter in the free Search & Discovery app. It appears on collection and search pages automatically.

Why don't product recommendations appear?

Related products need some sales history before Shopify can suggest them, and complementary products are chosen by you in the Search & Discovery app. The section stays hidden until there's something to show.

Can I use my own fonts?

Choose from Shopify's font library in Theme settings → Typography. Uploading custom font files requires editing code, which isn't covered by theme support.

Can I turn off the animations?

Yes. Turn off Theme settings → Motion and navigation → Animate the page. Visitors who've asked their device to reduce motion never see animations either way.

How do I link the menu to sections on my home page?

Use a link that starts with /#, such as /#treatments or /#book. See Header and footer for the full list.

How do I hide the bottom bar on phones?

Turn off Theme settings → Motion and navigation → Show the action bar on phones.

How do I update Lumen to a new version?

When an update is available, Shopify shows it in Online Store → Themes. Updating adds a new copy of the theme with your settings carried over; review it, then publish. Changes you made to theme code aren't carried over.

Is Lumen suitable for medical advertising rules?

Lumen gives you places for disclaimers, practitioner registrations and consent notes, but the wording you publish is your responsibility. Check the rules that apply to your clinic and country.

Can I use Lumen on more than one store?

Each purchase covers one store. To use Lumen on another store, buy it again from that store.

Support policy

Support is included with every Lumen purchase. We reply within two business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays).

Open the support form

What's included

  • Help using Lumen's settings, sections and blocks.
  • Fixes for bugs in the theme, such as broken layouts, dead links or features not working as described in this guide.
  • Guidance on setting up the Shopify features Lumen supports, such as metafields, filters and pickup.

What's not included

  • Custom design or code changes, including changes to theme files.
  • Problems caused by code you or someone else has added, including Custom Liquid sections and blocks.
  • Setting up or fixing third-party apps. Please contact the app's developer.

We don't sell paid add-on services with Lumen. For custom work, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner.

Code customizations

Code examples in this guide or from our support team are provided as a courtesy and aren't supported. Before editing code, duplicate your theme and make changes on the copy. If you're not comfortable editing code, hire a Shopify Partner.

When you contact us

Include your store URL (for example yourclinic.myshopify.com), a description of the problem and, if you can, a screenshot. It helps us answer in one reply.
